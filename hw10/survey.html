Commentary & Conclusions

The survey shows that e-commerce is highly popular: 90% of respondents have purchased online, with most buying monthly or weekly. Younger age groups (18–39) dominate online shopping, while Amazon is the clear favorite site. Education level correlates with higher online shopping frequency.

These findings align with broader statistics. According to Pew Internet, over 80% of U.S. adults have made online purchases. eMarketer reports that global e-commerce sales exceeded $5 trillion in 2022, with continued growth expected. ClickZ and E-Commerce Times highlight convenience, variety, and competitive pricing as key drivers of adoption.
